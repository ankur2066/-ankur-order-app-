Privacy Policy — Ankur Dryice

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy describes information handled by the Ankur Dryice mobile application and order services operated by Ankur Dryice and Events, Lucknow, India.

Information used

When you request a booking or use an account feature, the app may process your name, mobile number, delivery address or city, order quantity, order and payment-verification status, tracking identifiers, account identifier, language preference and notification token. Information is used to accept and fulfil requests, show order history and tracking, provide customer support, protect the service and send requested service notifications.

Service providers and sharing

Firebase services provide authentication, database, file storage and app-protection functions. Google sign-in may process account information under Google's terms. Order details may be available to the business owner and authorized staff who need them to fulfil an order. The business does not sell personal information. A customer may open WhatsApp or the phone dialer from contact buttons; information is sent to those services only when the customer chooses to continue there.

Payments

The app may record a selected payment method and whether payment was verified. Do not enter card number, UPI PIN, password or banking credentials in the app or chat. Payment verification is handled by the business and its payment provider outside the app.

Retention and requests

Order and accounting records are retained while needed to fulfil orders, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations. For access, correction or deletion requests, contact the business using the phone or WhatsApp number below. Some order records may need to be retained for legal or accounting reasons. The app currently does not state an automatic in-app account-deletion flow; contact support to submit a request.

Security and children's privacy

Access controls and service-provider safeguards are used, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. The app is intended for business customers and is not directed to children under 13. Do not submit a child's personal data unless it is necessary to fulfil an order and you are authorized to do so.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when app behavior changes. Contact Ankur Dryice and Events, Lucknow: +91 95808 82066 or WhatsApp +91 88963 32066.
