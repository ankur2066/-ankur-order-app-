Privacy Policy

This policy describes information used to receive and fulfil dry ice orders. We may collect the name, mobile number, delivery city/address, quantity, dispatch choice, order status and payment-verification notes that you provide.

We use these details to process orders, coordinate collection or delivery, answer support requests and maintain business records. The app stores order data in Firebase-hosted Firestore; access is restricted by account role and order-specific rules. We do not store UPI credentials or verify payments automatically. UPI transfers are made in the customer’s payment app and are checked by the owner.

Contact us to request correction or deletion where we are not required to retain an order record.
