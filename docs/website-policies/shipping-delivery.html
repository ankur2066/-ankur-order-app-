Shipping and Delivery Policy

Lucknow orders are coordinated for local delivery or collection as agreed at booking. Orders sent elsewhere in Uttar Pradesh are transported by bus where available. The customer is responsible for collecting the parcel from the agreed bus stop or bus depot.

The dispatch date and morning/evening slot shown on the order are estimates and may be affected by stock, transport or safety conditions. We contact the customer if the agreed plan changes.
