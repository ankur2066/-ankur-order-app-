## Full description

### English

Ankur Dryice helps customers request dry ice for weddings, events, packing and other business needs across Uttar Pradesh. Create a booking request, review its status, and follow delivery updates from My Orders. Use the ice calculator for an estimate and explore event video ideas. Contact the business directly for availability and final pricing.

Available customer tools include order history, order tracking, dry-ice handling guidance, service-area information, support links, and optional account features when enabled by the owner. Pyro services or products are shown only when specifically approved and enabled by the business. Availability and delivery timing are confirmed by the business for each request.
